# Trainer guide

Sign in at `/trainer/login` with the ID or email and password issued by Admin. Contact the office for credential assistance.

The dashboard shows your assigned students and evaluation queue. My assigned students is the default filter; All students exposes all academic profiles. Assignment routes work and notifications but does not restrict which students you may review. Student contact details and account/access controls are unavailable to Trainers.

Open an FLT marksheet to inspect submitted answers, objective scores, available Writing AI feedback and previous score revisions. A reviewer-presence message indicates another staff member is working on it. Coordinate with that reviewer; a stale save must be reloaded rather than overwriting newer work.

Enter valid section bands in half-band steps. Add remarks whenever changing a score. Save draft retains proposed marks without changing approved scores. Complete review saves the final approved section values and history. Failed AI evaluation can be retried when the provider is configured; manual review remains available.

Conduct Speaking offline. Enter the test date and whole-number 0-9 marks for Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation. The portal calculates the Speaking band. Any later change requires remarks. Students never record Speaking in this portal.

An FLT can be released by Admin with Speaking pending after L/R/W review. The result then shows a labelled three-skill overall. When Speaking is entered, it updates to four skills and the student receives a notification. Trainers cannot release results or change account credentials, enrollment, access periods, test unlocks or system settings.

Review your notifications and profile to track assigned work. Actual scoring decisions and remarks are audited under your authenticated identity, including work on another Trainer's assigned students.

Academic Reports provides server-calculated filters, performance comparisons, objective question-type accuracy, workload/turnaround and academic-only CSV export. Contacts are never included. The evaluation queue can filter course, assessment type, routed Trainer and status. Practice marksheets are also available through student academic history and reports.

Module Writing can be reviewed on its own; do not enter unrelated skill bands. Speaking fields apply only to FLTs. The Reload marksheet action discards unsaved edits and loads the current version when another reviewer/AI update caused a conflict. Original automatic/AI values, criteria, feedback and saved revision actor/role/time remain visible.
